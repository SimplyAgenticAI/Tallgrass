Let AI sort my Messenger chats

Off, chats are labelled by a word list in your browser and no message text ever leaves it — which is how "as much as I'd love to purchase… I'm broke" can look like an opportunity. On, the AI reads each waiting chat's preview line when you scan, and the recent messages of a chat you open, and labels it Opportunity, Question, Not now or nothing. The text is sent to the AI provider once and not stored; only the label and a few-word reason are kept. Each scan uses one AI draft from your allowance per 60 chats. Privacy details.
