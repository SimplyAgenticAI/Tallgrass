    Messages

    Messenger chats where someone is waiting on you, ones you've answered, and conversations that went quiet after your last message. Chats move between these on their own as you and they reply. {% if inbox.waiting or inbox.replied or inbox.quiet %} {{ inbox.waiting|length }} waiting{% if inbox.opportunities %}, {{ inbox.opportunities }} that look like opportunities{% endif %} · {{ inbox.replied|length }} replied · {{ inbox.quiet|length }} gone quiet. {% endif %}

    How it works. On facebook.com/messages, pick how many chats to read and press Find unanswered chats in the {{ app_name }} panel. Then open any chat and press ✨ Suggest a message: the next message is written into Messenger's box for you to edit and send. {{ app_name }} never sends a message. When you send one with the chat open, {{ app_name }} sees it and moves the chat to Replied by itself — no Done needed. Replies sent elsewhere, like your phone, are picked up the next time you find chats.

    What is kept: who each chat is with, its link, who spoke last and roughly when — never what anyone wrote. A conversation is sent to the AI only when you ask for a draft, and isn't stored.
